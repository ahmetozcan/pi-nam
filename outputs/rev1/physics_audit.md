## 4. Monotonicity of the submitted PI-NAM V1 (R2-7)

The penalty λ·E[ReLU(∂p̂/∂x_c)] (λ = 0.081) is **soft**. Out-of-fold check on the saved
15-seed ensembles (728 test days; `physics_monotonicity.csv`); FD = probability change when the
channel is raised by one standard deviation on the decision day:

| Channel | ∂p̂/∂x > 0 | FD increase > 0 | FD increase > 0.01 | max FD increase | median FD change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| windgust | 14.8 % | 16.5 % | 7.7 % | 0.138 | −0.029 |
| windspeed | 23.5 % | 31.9 % | 19.8 % | 0.139 | −0.011 |
| cloudcover | 7.3 % | 7.7 % | 4.1 % | 0.150 | −0.044 |
| precip | 17.7 % | 15.7 % | 2.9 % | 0.021 | −0.003 |
| snow | 0.0 % | 0.0 % | 0.0 % | 0.000 | −0.014 |

The manuscript's statement that the penalty "guarantees physically consistent monotone
behaviour" is therefore not correct: monotonicity holds on average (negative median
effects) but is violated locally, most for wind speed. A plausible mechanism is that wind
speed also enters through the gust factor (gust/speed, decreasing in speed) and the
interaction terms.

## 5. Justification of the monotonicity priors (R2-7)

| Channel | Direction | Basis | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| windspeed, windgust | non-increasing | SHT-OPS-B Art. 13(6)–(7): no hot-air balloon flight with surface wind above 10 kt (passenger) at take-off or forecast at landing; FAA (2024) Balloon Flying Handbook pp. 3-2–3-3: most pilots launch in winds below 7 kt and risk grows with wind speed | General physical + regulatory; daily maxima are a proxy for dawn wind (setting-specific approximation) |
| precip, snow | non-increasing | FAA (2024) pp. 3-2–3-3: flying in precipitation is bad practice; rain or frozen precipitation makes the envelope heavier and is associated with instability, gusts and downdrafts | General physical; daily totals may fall outside the dawn window (approximation) |
| cloudcover | non-increasing | SHT-OPS-B Art. 13(1)(b): visibility and cloud-separation minima (2,000 m; 450 m vertical, 600 m horizontal) and no flight in thermals or cumulonimbus; FAA (2024) Ch. 4: low clouds and restricted visibility in low-pressure systems | Approximation specific to this setting: total cloud cover does not distinguish high, harmless cloud from low ceilings |

Related, also supported by FAA (2024) p. 4-25: radiation fog forms with clear sky, a
narrow temperature–dew-point spread and light wind (< 7 kt), which is the structure of the
`fog_risk` index.

## 6. Decision process (supports R2-2 / R3-1)

SHT-OPS-B Art. 23(1)(c): the meteorological assessment for a balloon flight zone starts one
day before the flight with the meteorological reports and is finalised on the flight day
before flight time; a red flag may already be issued the day before. Art. 24(2)–(3): flags
are red (no flight), yellow (prepare, no flight) and green (flight permitted; advisory, final
responsibility with pilot and operator), published by the SHM. MGM has provided 72-hour WRF
wind and temperature forecasts for the Cappadocia flight zone since 2015-09-08.
